About

Why CorrectWay exists.

Finishing 12th grade means picking a direction with very little to go on — a handful of subjects you did or didn't enjoy, some advice from relatives, and a long list of career names that all sound equally vague. CorrectWay turns that guesswork into a structured, transparent analysis.

How the scoring works

You answer 25 yes/no questions. Each question is tagged with one or two of 12 underlying strength categories — analytical thinking, creativity, communication, hands-on skill, empathy, organization, technology, leadership, writing, numbers, science, and design. Every "yes" adds a point to the categories that question maps to.

Each of the 20 career profiles has its own weighting across those same categories. Your category scores are compared against every profile's weights to produce a percentage fit, and the top three are shown to you — each paired with one AI tool suited to exploring that path further.

Rule-based, not a black box

There's no external AI model call involved in the scoring itself — it's deterministic arithmetic over transparent weights, so the same 25 answers always produce the same result, and you can see exactly why a career was suggested rather than just being told that it was.
