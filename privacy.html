What we collect

If you register, we store your name, email address, and a securely hashed password. If you take the analysis while logged in, we store your answers and resulting career matches so you can review them later on your History page. If you use the Contact form, we store your name, email, and message.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share your data with third parties for advertising. We don't run your quiz answers through any external AI service — scoring happens entirely on this server.

Your choices

You can update your name from your Profile page. To request deletion of your account and associated quiz history, use the Contact form.
